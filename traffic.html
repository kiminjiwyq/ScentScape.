Green Buffers
Green Buffers

Green buffers act as natural bio-filters between industrial zones, high-emission waste management plants, and residential locations.

Framework Details:
  • Densely planted shrubs and multi-layered vegetation trap particulate matter PM 2.5 and PM 10 carrying odors.
  • Separating foul-smelling facilities from parks and community centers with physical barriers.
  • Utilizing resilient native species requiring minimal chemical treatment.
Roadside Trees
Roadside Trees

Integrating extensive tree canopies directly adjacent to busy roads counters vehicular smog and local tailpipe exhaust scents.

Framework Details:
  • Tree leaves absorb gaseous air pollutants such as nitrogen dioxide NO2, sulfur dioxide SO2, and carbon monoxide CO.
  • Reduces urban heat island effects, mitigating the decomposition rate of organic trash nearby.
  • Generates fresh air streams to continuously disperse localized pocket smells..
Redesign Pedestrian Paths
Redesign Pedestrian Paths

Redesigning the walkways ensures pedestrians are insulated from directly inhaling street-level pollutants and drainage odors.

Framework Details:
  • Pulling sidewalks further away from active traffic lanes to lower immediate toxin exposure.
  • Implementing rain gardens next to footpaths to prevent stagnant, smelly wastewater build-up.
  • Utilizing active materials that prevent toxic bacterial growth pools post-rainfall.
Low-emission Zones
Low-emission Zones

Enforcing strict zones where high-pollution and heavy diesel commercial vehicles are restricted drastically limits hazardous smell compounds.

Framework Details:
  • Implementing automatic license plate recognition (ALPR) cameras to enforce vehicle standards.
  • Schedule heavy freight and waste removal for nighttime.
  • Reducing congestion fees for zero-emission or electric delivery fleets.
Improve Traffic Flow
Improve Traffic Flow

Stop-and-go traffic multiplies vehicle emissions compared to steady driving. Improving traffic flow clears the air much faster.

Framework Details:
  • Using AI adaptive traffic lights to minimize idle wait times dynamically.
  • Replacing faulty road intersections with roundabouts to guarantee continuous, lower-intensity movement.
  • Preventing delivery drop-offs from blocking single-lane avenues.
Public Transport Routes
Public Transport Routes

Optimizing the efficiency and availability of mass public transport directly lowers private single-occupancy vehicle usage across the city.

Framework Details:
  • Transitioning public bus lines from old diesel setups to hydrogen fuel-cell (hydrogen and oyxgen to electricity) or electric alternatives.
  • Keeping public travel fast and predictable to promote public transportation.
  • Connecting primary bus/train exits directly to bike-sharing and walkable paths.
