Urban Smellscape

Smell is an important part of how people experience urban life. A clean-looking city may still feel uncomfortable if it has bad smells.

Sustainable

Improves air and smell quality using natural solutions like plants and green spaces.

Smart

Uses sensors and data to monitor bad smells and guide where greenery is needed.

City

Improves everyday urban spaces like streets, markets, and parks, making the city more pleasant to live in.

Urban Case Studies

Positive Sensory Zones

Examples of successful municipal design where environmental odors are actively managed.

Eco
Botanical Buffers

Using specific thick roadside tree barriers to neutralize traffic odors and industrial emissions before they reach neighborhoods.

Sensor
Smart Drainage Links

Automated structural flushing systems that trace stagnant water lines and eliminate sewer odors instantly.

Market
Ventilated Food Hubs

Redesigning open public markets with built-in sub-floor waste traps and rapid cooling to preserve delicate organic smellscapes.

Health
Olfactory Greenways

Urban pedestrian corridors cultivated with aromatic flora to intentionally generate stress-reducing, happy public spaces.

Impact on Community & Well-being

"In the market near my neighborhood, the smell can become very strong during the afternoon. There are many food stalls, wet waste, and trash bags left near the walkway. Sometimes the smell of spoiled food, fish, and garbage mixes together, which makes the area uncomfortable. Even though the market is useful for daily shopping, the bad smell makes people want to leave quickly."

Annoymous
Local Resident

"Poorly managed trash areas can negatively affect public health, emotional well-being, and people’s connection to the city. Smelly and polluted places show that waste systems are not being properly managed, which can make the environment uncomfortable and unhealthy. As a result, people may feel less safe, less happy, and less willing to live in or spend time in those areas."

Mya Thet Hmue
Urban Planner

"Near my house, the drains sometimes give off a bad smell, especially after heavy rain. The smell spreads around the street and makes the environment feel unhealthy. It affects how people feel about living there because even if the area looks normal, the smell makes it feel polluted and unpleasant. I think better cleaning and waste management would make the place more comfortable."

Annoymous
Local Resident
